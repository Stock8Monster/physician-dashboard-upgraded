## 🧠 Core Revolutionary Systems

### 1. **Ambient Clinical Intelligence System** 
*Zero-Click Documentation Revolution*

The system that makes documentation disappear. Our Ambient AI listens to entire consultations and generates complete, billable documentation without any physician input.

**Key Capabilities:**
- 🎤 **Continuous Ambient Listening** - Captures entire consultation automatically
- 📝 **Real-time SOAP Note Generation** - Creates comprehensive documentation as you speak
- 🏥 **Medical Context Understanding** - Knows medical terminology, abbreviations, and context
- 💰 **Automatic ICD-10/CPT Coding** - Generates billing codes with 95%+ accuracy
- 🔊 **Speaker Diarization** - Distinguishes between physician, patient, and other speakers

**File:** `src/services/ambientIntelligence.js`

### 2. **Predictive Patient Deterioration Engine**
*See The Future Before It Happens*

Our ML-powered system predicts patient deterioration 48-72 hours in advance with unprecedented accuracy.

**Predictive Capabilities:**
- ⚠️ **Sepsis Prediction** - 6-72 hour advance warning with SOFA/qSOFA scoring
- ❤️ **Cardiac Event Prediction** - MI, CHF, arrhythmia detection before symptoms
- 🫁 **Respiratory Failure** - Identifies risk before clinical signs
- 🧠 **Stroke Risk** - Real-time stroke probability assessment
- 📊 **Composite Risk Score** - Unified risk assessment across all conditions

**Digital Twin Technology:**
- Creates a complete physiological simulation of each patient
- Runs "what-if" scenarios for treatment options
- Simulates drug interactions and treatment outcomes
- Provides personalized risk trajectories

**File:** `src/services/predictiveAnalytics.js`
